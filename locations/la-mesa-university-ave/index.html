Questions About La Mesa - University Ave

We're at 7180 University Ave in La Mesa, at University Ave & Massachusetts. We're a 3-minute walk from Better Buzz, across from Midas, and just off the 94 Massachusetts exit; head north about 3 minutes. Locals may remember us as the old A1 Rental shop.

We're open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Early bird specials with 15% off are available before 9 AM.

Vapes, edibles, gummies, and flower are our top categories. We carry UP, Lights Out (flower and edibles), City Roots, and Lost Farm. Popular strains include classic OGs, Gorilla Glue, Sour Diesel, Girl Scout Cookies, Blue Dream, and Pink Acai.
