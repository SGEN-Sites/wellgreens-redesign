La historia

Time Machine centers its Wellgreens collection on cannabis flower and multi-pack pre-rolls, with several products built around Blue Dream, Wedding Cake, and Cereal Milk. The pre-roll lineup includes compact 7-packs at 3.5g and larger 28-packs at 14g, giving you two clearly different pack sizes to browse. Blue Dream is labeled Sativa and Wedding Cake is labeled Indica, while Cereal Milk appears throughout the collection without needing to narrow your search to a single format. For flower shoppers, the selection also includes 1 oz smalls in Blue Dream, Wedding Cake, Cereal Milk, and GG4. The collection has a strong multi-pack focus, but flower has its own place in the Time Machine lineup. Along with the 1 oz smalls, Wellgreens Mission Valley carries Wedding Cake Flower in a 14g format. We also carry Time Machine across all 10 Wellgreens stores, although the exact mix changes by location. Whether you're browsing cannabis flowers or looking for pre-roll packs with more than a few pieces, Time Machine gives you several sizes and strains to compare.
